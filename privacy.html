Privacy Policy

Last updated May 15, 2025

Information We Collect

Peapod does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. We do not track you, and we do not analyze your behavior in the app.

External Services We Use

Peapod uses Apple’s CloudKit framework to sync your podcast subscriptions and playback progress across your devices via your personal iCloud account. This data is stored privately in your iCloud and is only accessible to you. Peapod does not have access to this information, and it is not shared with any third parties.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data ourselves, there is no data for us to modify or delete. However, you can manage your iCloud data through your Apple device settings. If you have any concerns, feel free to contact us at support@peapod.fm.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with the updated date.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at support@peapod.fm.
